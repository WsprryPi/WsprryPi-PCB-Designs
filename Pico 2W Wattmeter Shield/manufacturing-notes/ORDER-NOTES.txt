PICO 2W WATTMETER SHIELD — CURRENT REQUIREMENTS, 2026-10-13
Two copper layers; nominal board thickness 1.6 mm.
Layout is placed and routed; ANALOG_3V3 connects the LDO, detector and ADC.
Experimental system: assembled RF, noise, fit and solder-process performance
are unqualified. The repository README and MIT warranty notice apply.

specify epoxy filling/capping for all seven 0.25 mm vias, and cross-check the
coordinate drawing. Leave ordinary 0.20 mm, 0.30 mm, and 0.40 mm vias unfilled.

VIA SETTINGS
Available copper diameter / drill: 0.50/0.20, 0.60/0.25, 0.60/0.30, 0.80/0.40 mm.
Selected U40/DECL vias: 0.60/0.25 mm (0.175 mm nominal annular ring).
Default netclass remains 0.60/0.30 mm. Use through vias only.
A size preset does not automatically select filling/capping or mask treatment.
Micro, blind, and buried vias are disallowed on this two-layer board.
Board minima: 0.50 mm via diameter, 0.20 mm drill, 0.10 mm annular ring,
0.25 mm hole-to-hole clearance. U40's minimum drill-edge gap is 0.35 mm.
Keep the selected vias aligned with U40 pad 17, R40 pad 1, and C48 pad 1
within the detector assembly.

STENCIL
Preserve the four 0.65 x 0.65 mm U40 exposed-pad paste windows in F.Paste.
Do not substitute a solid/full-pad aperture. Confirm stencil thickness,
paste release and reflow process with the assembler.

ASSEMBLY
Current source marks 34 front-side (F.Cu) components for factory assembly:
C20-C21, C30-C34, C40-C50, C60-C61; R30-R34, R40, R50, R60-R63;
U20, U40, U60. This is 20 capacitors, 11 resistors, and 3 ICs.
BOM and placement exports must contain exactly these 34 factory references.
J30 SMA and J10/J11 female sockets are fitted by hand; U10 represents their
combined Pico interface on B.Cu. Omit these manual parts and the front logo
from factory assembly outputs. Retain all PCB pads and holes.
Use the LCSC Part # field. Check U20/U40/U60 pin-1 orientation in the supplier
preview against the front-side layout.
ASSEMBLY-FRONT.svg shows all 34 factory components and IC pad-1 markers.

MECHANICAL
Keep the antenna notch and copper keepout. Keep panel tabs/tooling away
from the SMA seating edge and antenna opening. The bare-board layout has
no dedicated fiducials; panel fiducials/tooling are supplier assembly requirements. The PCB copper-finish field is None;
specify a compatible surface finish in the order.
SMA: Adafruit 1865 for nominal 1.6 mm board.
Hand-fit sockets: 2.54 mm pitch, 17.78 mm row spacing, 1.00 mm holes.
Select mating height and lead dimensions for the actual Pico/header stack.

POWER
U20 requires VBUS from Pico pin 40 for the analog 3.3 V rail.
Powering the Pico solely through VSYS leaves U40/U60 and the analog rail unpowered.
