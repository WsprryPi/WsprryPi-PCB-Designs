PICO 2W WATTMETER SHIELD
Two copper layers; nominal board thickness 1.6 mm.

U1 VIA-IN-PAD - REQUIRED SELECTIVE PROCESS
Fill with nonconductive epoxy, planarize, and copper-cap ONLY the five
0.30 mm drilled vias inside U1 exposed GND pad 17 (IPC-4761 Type VII).
Provide a flat solderable top surface. Cover the rear pads of these five vias
with soldermask; keep the front paddle solderable. Retain epoxy filling and
copper capping on all five. All other vias retain their specified treatment. Do NOT fill all holes of the same diameter.
See U1-VIA-TREATMENT.svg for locations; coordinates are in board mm.
Confirm that the selected two-layer process supports this treatment.
Request production-file confirmation before manufacture.

STENCIL
Preserve the four 0.65 x 0.65 mm U1 exposed-pad paste windows in F.Paste.
Do not substitute a solid/full-pad aperture. Confirm stencil thickness,
paste release and reflow process with the assembler.

ASSEMBLY
Machine populate C1-C9, R1-R8, U1 and U2 only: 19 front-side components.
J1 SMA and J2/J3 female sockets are fitted by hand; U3 represents their
combined Pico interface footprint. Omit J1/J2/J3/U3 from supplier assembly BOM
and omit J1/U3 from machine placement. Retain all PCB pads and holes.
Use the LCSC Part # column to identify parts. Verify U1/U2 pin-1 orientation in
the supplier placement preview against ASSEMBLY-TOP.svg.

MECHANICAL
Keep the antenna notch and copper keepout. Keep panel tabs/tooling away
from the SMA seating edge and antenna opening. Supplier may add panel
fiducials/tooling; the bare-board layout has no dedicated fiducials.
SMA: Adafruit 1865 for nominal 1.6 mm board; no unverified substitution.
Hand-fit sockets: 2.54 mm pitch, 17.78 mm row spacing, 1.00 mm holes.
Select mating height and lead dimensions for the actual Pico/header stack.

STATUS
Development design; physical fit, RF calibration and solder process are
not yet qualified. No supplier order or process approval has been obtained.
